## Preamble

- This Financial Controls and Expense Policy establishes the standards Axon applies in the area of financial controls and expense.
- It applies to every employee, officer, director, contractor, consultant and temporary worker acting for Axon in any country.
- The Finance owns this document and is accountable for keeping it accurate, accessible and enforced.
- In this policy, 'must' denotes a mandatory requirement, 'should' denotes a strong recommendation, and 'may' denotes a permitted option.
- Where this policy conflicts with applicable law, the law prevails; where it is stricter than the law, this policy prevails.
- Questions about interpretation should be directed to the Policy Owner or the Compliance team.

## 1. Financial Governance

### 1.1 Overview

This section sets out how Axon manages **financial governance** within the framework of the Financial Controls and Expense Policy. It explains the objective, the expectations on individuals, and the evidence required to demonstrate compliance.

### 1.2 Requirements

1.2.1 Axon will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a good-faith concern about financial governance.
1.2.2 The internal audit function shall maintain financial governance annually and report the outcome to senior management.
1.2.3 Failure to follow the requirements for financial governance may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.
1.2.4 Evidence supporting financial governance must be retained according to the Records Retention Schedule and be available for audit.
1.2.5 Where financial governance involves a third party, department heads must confirm that the third party meets equivalent standards.
1.2.6 Decisions about financial governance must be proportionate, risk-based and consistent with Axon's values.
1.2.7 Axon expects every person involved in financial governance to act with honesty, diligence and transparency.
1.2.8 Contractors and temporary staff must escalate any significant change to financial governance and keep an auditable log entry of the change.
1.2.9 New hires and role changers must complete training covering financial governance within thirty days of start or transfer.
1.2.10 Where local law sets a stricter requirement for financial governance, the stricter requirement prevails in that jurisdiction.
1.2.11 Metrics for financial governance will be collected monthly and presented to the governance committee.
1.2.12 The executive leadership team are responsible for ensuring that financial governance is understood, applied consistently and evidenced with an entry in the central register.
1.2.13 Controls for financial governance shall be reviewed whenever material changes occur and updated where gaps or changes in risk are identified.
1.2.14 Department heads must report on all activities related to financial governance in accordance with this policy and applicable law.
1.2.15 Any exception relating to financial governance requires a signed approval approved by the Policy Owner before the activity proceeds.
1.2.16 Concerns relating to financial governance must be raised promptly through the Speak-Up channel, the manager, or the Compliance team.

### 1.3 Responsibilities

- **The policy owner** must test and validate financial governance at least once every twelve months.
- **The relevant control owner** must approve and document financial governance monthly.
- **The executive leadership team** must review and report on financial governance whenever material changes occur.
- **The legal department** must approve and monitor financial governance quarterly.
- **Department heads** must maintain and maintain financial governance whenever material changes occur.

### 1.4 Controls Checklist

- [ ] Confirm that financial governance is communicated and supported by a ticket in the case management system.
- [ ] Confirm that financial governance is reviewed and supported by a ticket in the case management system.
- [ ] Confirm that financial governance is monitored and supported by an auditable log entry.
- [ ] Confirm that financial governance is monitored and supported by a ticket in the case management system.
- [ ] Confirm that financial governance is tested and supported by a written record.
- [ ] Confirm that financial governance is escalated where needed and supported by a documented decision memo.
- [ ] Confirm that financial governance is approved and supported by an entry in the central register.
- [ ] Confirm that financial governance is documented and supported by a written record.

### 1.5 Guidance and Examples

- *Example 1:* An employee facing a question about financial governance should seek Compliance advice before acting, and should keep an auditable log entry of the outcome.
- *Example 2:* An employee facing a question about financial governance should consult their manager before acting, and should keep a documented decision memo of the outcome.
