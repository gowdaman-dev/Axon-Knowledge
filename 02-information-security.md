## Preamble

- This Information Security Policy establishes the standards Axon applies in the area of information security.
- It applies to every employee, officer, director, contractor, consultant and temporary worker acting for Axon in any country.
- The Information Security Office owns this document and is accountable for keeping it accurate, accessible and enforced.
- In this policy, 'must' denotes a mandatory requirement, 'should' denotes a strong recommendation, and 'may' denotes a permitted option.
- Where this policy conflicts with applicable law, the law prevails; where it is stricter than the law, this policy prevails.
- Questions about interpretation should be directed to the Policy Owner or the Compliance team.

## 1. Security Governance

### 1.1 Overview

This section sets out how Axon manages **security governance** within the framework of the Information Security Policy. It explains the objective, the expectations on individuals, and the evidence required to demonstrate compliance.

### 1.2 Requirements

1.2.1 Axon expects every person involved in security governance to act with honesty, diligence and transparency.
1.2.2 Evidence supporting security governance must be retained according to the Records Retention Schedule and be available for audit.
1.2.3 New hires and role changers must complete training covering security governance within thirty days of start or transfer.
1.2.4 The policy owner shall review security governance whenever material changes occur and report the outcome to senior management.
1.2.5 Failure to follow the requirements for security governance may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.
1.2.6 Where local law sets a stricter requirement for security governance, the stricter requirement prevails in that jurisdiction.
1.2.7 Where security governance involves a third party, line managers must confirm that the third party meets equivalent standards.
1.2.8 The policy owner are responsible for ensuring that security governance is understood, applied consistently and evidenced with an auditable log entry.
1.2.9 Controls for security governance shall be reviewed whenever material changes occur and updated where gaps or changes in risk are identified.
1.2.10 Concerns relating to security governance must be raised promptly through the Speak-Up channel, the manager, or the Compliance team.
1.2.11 Axon will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a good-faith concern about security governance.
1.2.12 Line managers must report on any significant change to security governance and keep a ticket in the case management system of the change.
1.2.13 Any exception relating to security governance requires a signed approval approved by the Policy Owner before the activity proceeds.
1.2.14 The compliance team must maintain all activities related to security governance in accordance with this policy and applicable law.
1.2.15 Metrics for security governance will be collected at least once every twelve months and presented to the governance committee.
1.2.16 Decisions about security governance must be proportionate, risk-based and consistent with Axon's values.

### 1.3 Responsibilities

- **The policy owner** must test and test security governance quarterly.
- **The legal department** must validate and approve security governance annually.
- **Contractors and temporary staff** must maintain and maintain security governance whenever material changes occur.
- **All employees** must escalate and communicate security governance quarterly.
- **Department heads** must escalate and validate security governance semi-annually.

### 1.4 Controls Checklist

- [ ] Confirm that security governance is escalated where needed and supported by an auditable log entry.
- [ ] Confirm that security governance is monitored and supported by an auditable log entry.
- [ ] Confirm that security governance is communicated and supported by a signed approval.
- [ ] Confirm that security governance is evidenced and supported by a signed approval.
- [ ] Confirm that security governance is documented and supported by an entry in the central register.
- [ ] Confirm that security governance is monitored and supported by a documented decision memo.
- [ ] Confirm that security governance is documented and supported by a written record.
- [ ] Confirm that security governance is approved and supported by an auditable log entry.

### 1.5 Guidance and Examples

- *Example 1:* An employee facing a question about security governance should raise a ticket before acting, and should keep a written record of the outcome.
- *Example 2:* An employee facing a question about security governance should read this section again before acting, and should keep a written record of the outcome.
- *Example 3:* An employee facing a question about security governance should consult their manager before acting, and should keep a written record of the outcome.
- *Example 4:* An employee facing a question about security governance should consult their manager before acting, and should keep an entry in the central register of the outcome.
- *Example 5:* An employee facing a question about security governance should contact the Policy Owner before acting, and should keep an entry in the central register of the outcome.
