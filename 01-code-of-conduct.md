## Preamble

- This Code of Conduct and Business Ethics establishes the standards Axon applies in the area of code of conduct and business ethics.
- It applies to every employee, officer, director, contractor, consultant and temporary worker acting for Axon in any country.
- The Human Resources & Compliance owns this document and is accountable for keeping it accurate, accessible and enforced.
- In this policy, 'must' denotes a mandatory requirement, 'should' denotes a strong recommendation, and 'may' denotes a permitted option.
- Where this policy conflicts with applicable law, the law prevails; where it is stricter than the law, this policy prevails.
- Questions about interpretation should be directed to the Policy Owner or the Compliance team.

## 1. Integrity in Daily Work

### 1.1 Overview

This section sets out how Axon manages **integrity in daily work** within the framework of the Code of Conduct and Business Ethics. It explains the objective, the expectations on individuals, and the evidence required to demonstrate compliance.

### 1.2 Requirements

1.2.1 Department heads shall escalate integrity in daily work quarterly and report the outcome to senior management.
1.2.2 Any exception relating to integrity in daily work requires an entry in the central register approved by the Policy Owner before the activity proceeds.
1.2.3 New hires and role changers must complete training covering integrity in daily work within thirty days of start or transfer.
1.2.4 Metrics for integrity in daily work will be collected at least once every twelve months and presented to the governance committee.
1.2.5 Evidence supporting integrity in daily work must be retained according to the Records Retention Schedule and be available for audit.
1.2.6 The policy owner are responsible for ensuring that integrity in daily work is understood, applied consistently and evidenced with a documented decision memo.
1.2.7 Controls for integrity in daily work shall be reviewed at least once every twelve months and updated where gaps or changes in risk are identified.
1.2.8 Failure to follow the requirements for integrity in daily work may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.
1.2.9 Axon will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a good-faith concern about integrity in daily work.
1.2.10 Where local law sets a stricter requirement for integrity in daily work, the stricter requirement prevails in that jurisdiction.
1.2.11 The executive leadership team must communicate all activities related to integrity in daily work in accordance with this policy and applicable law.
1.2.12 Axon expects every person involved in integrity in daily work to act with honesty, diligence and transparency.
1.2.13 Decisions about integrity in daily work must be proportionate, risk-based and consistent with Axon's values.
1.2.14 Concerns relating to integrity in daily work must be raised promptly through the Speak-Up channel, the manager, or the Compliance team.
1.2.15 Where integrity in daily work involves a third party, the Policy Owner must confirm that the third party meets equivalent standards.
1.2.16 The compliance team must maintain any significant change to integrity in daily work and keep a ticket in the case management system of the change.

### 1.3 Responsibilities

- **The executive leadership team** must report on and maintain integrity in daily work at least once every twelve months.
- **Contractors and temporary staff** must validate and report on integrity in daily work whenever material changes occur.
- **The policy owner** must document and report on integrity in daily work semi-annually.
- **The legal department** must approve and communicate integrity in daily work monthly.
- **Line managers** must maintain and document integrity in daily work quarterly.

### 1.4 Controls Checklist

- [ ] Confirm that integrity in daily work is reviewed and supported by an entry in the central register.
- [ ] Confirm that integrity in daily work is escalated where needed and supported by an auditable log entry.
- [ ] Confirm that integrity in daily work is approved and supported by a ticket in the case management system.
- [ ] Confirm that integrity in daily work is evidenced and supported by a written record.
- [ ] Confirm that integrity in daily work is communicated and supported by a signed approval.
- [ ] Confirm that integrity in daily work is escalated where needed and supported by a written record.
- [ ] Confirm that integrity in daily work is documented and supported by a documented decision memo.
- [ ] Confirm that integrity in daily work is approved and supported by a documented decision memo.

### 1.5 Guidance and Examples

- *Example 1:* An employee facing a question about integrity in daily work should consult their manager before acting, and should keep an entry in the central register of the outcome.
- *Example 2:* An employee facing a question about integrity in daily work should read this section again before acting, and should keep an auditable log entry of the outcome.
