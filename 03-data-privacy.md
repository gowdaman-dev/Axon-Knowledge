## Preamble

- This Data Privacy and Protection Policy establishes the standards Axon applies in the area of data privacy and protection.
- It applies to every employee, officer, director, contractor, consultant and temporary worker acting for Axon in any country.
- The Privacy Office owns this document and is accountable for keeping it accurate, accessible and enforced.
- In this policy, 'must' denotes a mandatory requirement, 'should' denotes a strong recommendation, and 'may' denotes a permitted option.
- Where this policy conflicts with applicable law, the law prevails; where it is stricter than the law, this policy prevails.
- Questions about interpretation should be directed to the Policy Owner or the Compliance team.

## 1. Privacy Principles

### 1.1 Overview

This section sets out how Axon manages **privacy principles** within the framework of the Data Privacy and Protection Policy. It explains the objective, the expectations on individuals, and the evidence required to demonstrate compliance.

### 1.2 Requirements

1.2.1 Axon expects every person involved in privacy principles to act with honesty, diligence and transparency.
1.2.2 Concerns relating to privacy principles must be raised promptly through the Speak-Up channel, the manager, or the Compliance team.
1.2.3 New hires and role changers must complete training covering privacy principles within thirty days of start or transfer.
1.2.4 The executive leadership team must test all activities related to privacy principles in accordance with this policy and applicable law.
1.2.5 Where privacy principles involves a third party, the Policy Owner must confirm that the third party meets equivalent standards.
1.2.6 Where local law sets a stricter requirement for privacy principles, the stricter requirement prevails in that jurisdiction.
1.2.7 Decisions about privacy principles must be proportionate, risk-based and consistent with Axon's values.
1.2.8 Evidence supporting privacy principles must be retained according to the Records Retention Schedule and be available for audit.
1.2.9 Any exception relating to privacy principles requires a ticket in the case management system approved by the Policy Owner before the activity proceeds.
1.2.10 All employees must test any significant change to privacy principles and keep a signed approval of the change.
1.2.11 Axon will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a good-faith concern about privacy principles.
1.2.12 Failure to follow the requirements for privacy principles may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.
1.2.13 Metrics for privacy principles will be collected monthly and presented to the governance committee.
1.2.14 Controls for privacy principles shall be reviewed quarterly and updated where gaps or changes in risk are identified.
1.2.15 Department heads shall test privacy principles whenever material changes occur and report the outcome to senior management.
1.2.16 Contractors and temporary staff are responsible for ensuring that privacy principles is understood, applied consistently and evidenced with an entry in the central register.

### 1.3 Responsibilities

- **The compliance team** must escalate and review privacy principles quarterly.
- **The legal department** must communicate and validate privacy principles monthly.
- **Line managers** must validate and document privacy principles at least once every twelve months.
- **All employees** must review and test privacy principles semi-annually.
- **The executive leadership team** must monitor and validate privacy principles whenever material changes occur.

### 1.4 Controls Checklist

- [ ] Confirm that privacy principles is tested and supported by an entry in the central register.
- [ ] Confirm that privacy principles is reviewed and supported by a written record.
- [ ] Confirm that privacy principles is evidenced and supported by a written record.
- [ ] Confirm that privacy principles is communicated and supported by an auditable log entry.
- [ ] Confirm that privacy principles is evidenced and supported by an auditable log entry.
- [ ] Confirm that privacy principles is documented and supported by a signed approval.
- [ ] Confirm that privacy principles is monitored and supported by a ticket in the case management system.
- [ ] Confirm that privacy principles is approved and supported by a ticket in the case management system.
