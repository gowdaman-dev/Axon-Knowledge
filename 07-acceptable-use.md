## Preamble

- This Acceptable Use Policy establishes the standards Axon applies in the area of acceptable use.
- It applies to every employee, officer, director, contractor, consultant and temporary worker acting for Axon in any country.
- The IT Operations owns this document and is accountable for keeping it accurate, accessible and enforced.
- In this policy, 'must' denotes a mandatory requirement, 'should' denotes a strong recommendation, and 'may' denotes a permitted option.
- Where this policy conflicts with applicable law, the law prevails; where it is stricter than the law, this policy prevails.
- Questions about interpretation should be directed to the Policy Owner or the Compliance team.

## 1. Scope of Use

### 1.1 Overview

This section sets out how Axon manages **scope of use** within the framework of the Acceptable Use Policy. It explains the objective, the expectations on individuals, and the evidence required to demonstrate compliance.

### 1.2 Requirements

1.2.1 Where local law sets a stricter requirement for scope of use, the stricter requirement prevails in that jurisdiction.
1.2.2 Contractors and temporary staff shall communicate scope of use monthly and report the outcome to senior management.
1.2.3 Evidence supporting scope of use must be retained according to the Records Retention Schedule and be available for audit.
1.2.4 New hires and role changers must complete training covering scope of use within thirty days of start or transfer.
1.2.5 Concerns relating to scope of use must be raised promptly through the Speak-Up channel, the manager, or the Compliance team.
1.2.6 Controls for scope of use shall be reviewed annually and updated where gaps or changes in risk are identified.
1.2.7 The executive leadership team must monitor any significant change to scope of use and keep a ticket in the case management system of the change.
1.2.8 The policy owner are responsible for ensuring that scope of use is understood, applied consistently and evidenced with a documented decision memo.
1.2.9 Any exception relating to scope of use requires a ticket in the case management system approved by the Policy Owner before the activity proceeds.
1.2.10 Decisions about scope of use must be proportionate, risk-based and consistent with Axon's values.
1.2.11 Where scope of use involves a third party, the Policy Owner must confirm that the third party meets equivalent standards.
1.2.12 Axon expects every person involved in scope of use to act with honesty, diligence and transparency.
1.2.13 Metrics for scope of use will be collected monthly and presented to the governance committee.
1.2.14 Axon will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a good-faith concern about scope of use.
1.2.15 Failure to follow the requirements for scope of use may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.
1.2.16 Department heads must report on all activities related to scope of use in accordance with this policy and applicable law.

### 1.3 Responsibilities

- **The legal department** must approve and review scope of use whenever material changes occur.
- **Department heads** must escalate and test scope of use monthly.
- **Contractors and temporary staff** must test and test scope of use semi-annually.
- **The compliance team** must document and monitor scope of use whenever material changes occur.
- **The policy owner** must test and communicate scope of use whenever material changes occur.

### 1.4 Controls Checklist

- [ ] Confirm that scope of use is reviewed and supported by an entry in the central register.
- [ ] Confirm that scope of use is evidenced and supported by an auditable log entry.
- [ ] Confirm that scope of use is approved and supported by a ticket in the case management system.
- [ ] Confirm that scope of use is monitored and supported by an entry in the central register.
- [ ] Confirm that scope of use is monitored and supported by a ticket in the case management system.
- [ ] Confirm that scope of use is escalated where needed and supported by a signed approval.
- [ ] Confirm that scope of use is reviewed and supported by an entry in the central register.
- [ ] Confirm that scope of use is monitored and supported by a documented decision memo.
