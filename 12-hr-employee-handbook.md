## Preamble

- This Employee Conduct, Leave and Performance Policy establishes the standards Axon applies in the area of employee conduct, leave and performance.
- It applies to every employee, officer, director, contractor, consultant and temporary worker acting for Axon in any country.
- The Human Resources owns this document and is accountable for keeping it accurate, accessible and enforced.
- In this policy, 'must' denotes a mandatory requirement, 'should' denotes a strong recommendation, and 'may' denotes a permitted option.
- Where this policy conflicts with applicable law, the law prevails; where it is stricter than the law, this policy prevails.
- Questions about interpretation should be directed to the Policy Owner or the Compliance team.

## 1. Employment Basics

### 1.1 Overview

This section sets out how Axon manages **employment basics** within the framework of the Employee Conduct, Leave and Performance Policy. It explains the objective, the expectations on individuals, and the evidence required to demonstrate compliance.

### 1.2 Requirements

1.2.1 Evidence supporting employment basics must be retained according to the Records Retention Schedule and be available for audit.
1.2.2 Where local law sets a stricter requirement for employment basics, the stricter requirement prevails in that jurisdiction.
1.2.3 Where employment basics involves a third party, the Executive Leadership Team must confirm that the third party meets equivalent standards.
1.2.4 The policy owner are responsible for ensuring that employment basics is understood, applied consistently and evidenced with an entry in the central register.
1.2.5 Controls for employment basics shall be reviewed whenever material changes occur and updated where gaps or changes in risk are identified.
1.2.6 Failure to follow the requirements for employment basics may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.
1.2.7 Concerns relating to employment basics must be raised promptly through the Speak-Up channel, the manager, or the Compliance team.
1.2.8 Axon expects every person involved in employment basics to act with honesty, diligence and transparency.
1.2.9 New hires and role changers must complete training covering employment basics within thirty days of start or transfer.
1.2.10 The relevant control owner must report on all activities related to employment basics in accordance with this policy and applicable law.
1.2.11 The compliance team must report on any significant change to employment basics and keep an entry in the central register of the change.
1.2.12 Any exception relating to employment basics requires a documented decision memo approved by the Policy Owner before the activity proceeds.
1.2.13 Metrics for employment basics will be collected monthly and presented to the governance committee.
1.2.14 Decisions about employment basics must be proportionate, risk-based and consistent with Axon's values.
1.2.15 Axon will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a good-faith concern about employment basics.
1.2.16 Contractors and temporary staff shall validate employment basics at least once every twelve months and report the outcome to senior management.

### 1.3 Responsibilities

- **All employees** must review and escalate employment basics monthly.
- **The executive leadership team** must document and report on employment basics at least once every twelve months.
- **The legal department** must maintain and report on employment basics whenever material changes occur.
- **Line managers** must document and approve employment basics monthly.
- **The compliance team** must validate and monitor employment basics quarterly.

### 1.4 Controls Checklist

- [ ] Confirm that employment basics is evidenced and supported by an entry in the central register.
- [ ] Confirm that employment basics is approved and supported by a written record.
- [ ] Confirm that employment basics is reviewed and supported by a ticket in the case management system.
- [ ] Confirm that employment basics is monitored and supported by a ticket in the case management system.
- [ ] Confirm that employment basics is documented and supported by a documented decision memo.
- [ ] Confirm that employment basics is evidenced and supported by a documented decision memo.
- [ ] Confirm that employment basics is reviewed and supported by a ticket in the case management system.
- [ ] Confirm that employment basics is documented and supported by a documented decision memo.

### 1.5 Guidance and Examples

- *Example 1:* An employee facing a question about employment basics should seek Compliance advice before acting, and should keep an entry in the central register of the outcome.
- *Example 2:* An employee facing a question about employment basics should raise a ticket before acting, and should keep an auditable log entry of the outcome.
- *Example 3:* An employee facing a question about employment basics should check the intranet FAQ before acting, and should keep an entry in the central register of the outcome.
