## Preamble

- This Procurement and Vendor Management Policy establishes the standards Axon applies in the area of procurement and vendor management.
- It applies to every employee, officer, director, contractor, consultant and temporary worker acting for Axon in any country.
- The Procurement owns this document and is accountable for keeping it accurate, accessible and enforced.
- In this policy, 'must' denotes a mandatory requirement, 'should' denotes a strong recommendation, and 'may' denotes a permitted option.
- Where this policy conflicts with applicable law, the law prevails; where it is stricter than the law, this policy prevails.
- Questions about interpretation should be directed to the Policy Owner or the Compliance team.

## 1. Procurement Principles

### 1.1 Overview

This section sets out how Axon manages **procurement principles** within the framework of the Procurement and Vendor Management Policy. It explains the objective, the expectations on individuals, and the evidence required to demonstrate compliance.

### 1.2 Requirements

1.2.1 Where procurement principles involves a third party, the Legal department must confirm that the third party meets equivalent standards.
1.2.2 Axon expects every person involved in procurement principles to act with honesty, diligence and transparency.
1.2.3 Failure to follow the requirements for procurement principles may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.
1.2.4 New hires and role changers must complete training covering procurement principles within thirty days of start or transfer.
1.2.5 Line managers shall maintain procurement principles monthly and report the outcome to senior management.
1.2.6 All employees must review all activities related to procurement principles in accordance with this policy and applicable law.
1.2.7 Axon will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a good-faith concern about procurement principles.
1.2.8 Concerns relating to procurement principles must be raised promptly through the Speak-Up channel, the manager, or the Compliance team.
1.2.9 Decisions about procurement principles must be proportionate, risk-based and consistent with Axon's values.
1.2.10 The legal department must maintain any significant change to procurement principles and keep a ticket in the case management system of the change.
1.2.11 Where local law sets a stricter requirement for procurement principles, the stricter requirement prevails in that jurisdiction.
1.2.12 The internal audit function are responsible for ensuring that procurement principles is understood, applied consistently and evidenced with a signed approval.
1.2.13 Metrics for procurement principles will be collected monthly and presented to the governance committee.
1.2.14 Any exception relating to procurement principles requires an entry in the central register approved by the Policy Owner before the activity proceeds.
1.2.15 Controls for procurement principles shall be reviewed at least once every twelve months and updated where gaps or changes in risk are identified.
1.2.16 Evidence supporting procurement principles must be retained according to the Records Retention Schedule and be available for audit.

### 1.3 Responsibilities

- **Line managers** must approve and document procurement principles quarterly.
- **The relevant control owner** must report on and monitor procurement principles quarterly.
- **The compliance team** must validate and escalate procurement principles at least once every twelve months.
- **The policy owner** must communicate and validate procurement principles quarterly.
- **Contractors and temporary staff** must communicate and approve procurement principles semi-annually.

### 1.4 Controls Checklist

- [ ] Confirm that procurement principles is tested and supported by an auditable log entry.
- [ ] Confirm that procurement principles is tested and supported by a documented decision memo.
- [ ] Confirm that procurement principles is documented and supported by a signed approval.
- [ ] Confirm that procurement principles is monitored and supported by a ticket in the case management system.
- [ ] Confirm that procurement principles is tested and supported by a written record.
- [ ] Confirm that procurement principles is evidenced and supported by a documented decision memo.
- [ ] Confirm that procurement principles is reviewed and supported by an auditable log entry.
- [ ] Confirm that procurement principles is monitored and supported by an entry in the central register.

### 1.5 Guidance and Examples

- *Example 1:* An employee facing a question about procurement principles should read this section again before acting, and should keep an auditable log entry of the outcome.
- *Example 2:* An employee facing a question about procurement principles should contact the Policy Owner before acting, and should keep a ticket in the case management system of the outcome.
