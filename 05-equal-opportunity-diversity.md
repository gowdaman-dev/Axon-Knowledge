## Preamble

- This Equal Opportunity, Diversity and Inclusion Policy establishes the standards Axon applies in the area of equal opportunity, diversity and inclusion.
- It applies to every employee, officer, director, contractor, consultant and temporary worker acting for Axon in any country.
- The Human Resources owns this document and is accountable for keeping it accurate, accessible and enforced.
- In this policy, 'must' denotes a mandatory requirement, 'should' denotes a strong recommendation, and 'may' denotes a permitted option.
- Where this policy conflicts with applicable law, the law prevails; where it is stricter than the law, this policy prevails.
- Questions about interpretation should be directed to the Policy Owner or the Compliance team.

## 1. Commitment to Equality

### 1.1 Overview

This section sets out how Axon manages **commitment to equality** within the framework of the Equal Opportunity, Diversity and Inclusion Policy. It explains the objective, the expectations on individuals, and the evidence required to demonstrate compliance.

### 1.2 Requirements

1.2.1 Axon will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a good-faith concern about commitment to equality.
1.2.2 Axon expects every person involved in commitment to equality to act with honesty, diligence and transparency.
1.2.3 Where local law sets a stricter requirement for commitment to equality, the stricter requirement prevails in that jurisdiction.
1.2.4 The relevant control owner must escalate all activities related to commitment to equality in accordance with this policy and applicable law.
1.2.5 Metrics for commitment to equality will be collected semi-annually and presented to the governance committee.
1.2.6 Controls for commitment to equality shall be reviewed monthly and updated where gaps or changes in risk are identified.
1.2.7 The internal audit function must document any significant change to commitment to equality and keep an auditable log entry of the change.
1.2.8 The policy owner are responsible for ensuring that commitment to equality is understood, applied consistently and evidenced with a documented decision memo.
1.2.9 Where commitment to equality involves a third party, all employees must confirm that the third party meets equivalent standards.
1.2.10 Evidence supporting commitment to equality must be retained according to the Records Retention Schedule and be available for audit.
1.2.11 New hires and role changers must complete training covering commitment to equality within thirty days of start or transfer.
1.2.12 The compliance team shall approve commitment to equality annually and report the outcome to senior management.
1.2.13 Concerns relating to commitment to equality must be raised promptly through the Speak-Up channel, the manager, or the Compliance team.
1.2.14 Any exception relating to commitment to equality requires an auditable log entry approved by the Policy Owner before the activity proceeds.
1.2.15 Decisions about commitment to equality must be proportionate, risk-based and consistent with Axon's values.
1.2.16 Failure to follow the requirements for commitment to equality may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.

### 1.3 Responsibilities

- **The executive leadership team** must monitor and escalate commitment to equality semi-annually.
- **The relevant control owner** must test and communicate commitment to equality monthly.
- **The internal audit function** must test and validate commitment to equality whenever material changes occur.
- **The legal department** must maintain and validate commitment to equality quarterly.
- **Contractors and temporary staff** must monitor and report on commitment to equality quarterly.

### 1.4 Controls Checklist

- [ ] Confirm that commitment to equality is reviewed and supported by a written record.
- [ ] Confirm that commitment to equality is evidenced and supported by a written record.
- [ ] Confirm that commitment to equality is communicated and supported by a documented decision memo.
- [ ] Confirm that commitment to equality is reviewed and supported by an auditable log entry.
- [ ] Confirm that commitment to equality is reviewed and supported by an entry in the central register.
- [ ] Confirm that commitment to equality is escalated where needed and supported by a signed approval.
- [ ] Confirm that commitment to equality is documented and supported by an entry in the central register.
- [ ] Confirm that commitment to equality is reviewed and supported by an entry in the central register.

### 1.5 Guidance and Examples

- *Example 1:* An employee facing a question about commitment to equality should read this section again before acting, and should keep a signed approval of the outcome.
- *Example 2:* An employee facing a question about commitment to equality should raise a ticket before acting, and should keep an auditable log entry of the outcome.
