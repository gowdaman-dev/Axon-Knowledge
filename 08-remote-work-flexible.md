## Preamble

- This Remote and Flexible Work Policy establishes the standards Axon applies in the area of remote and flexible work.
- It applies to every employee, officer, director, contractor, consultant and temporary worker acting for Axon in any country.
- The Human Resources & IT owns this document and is accountable for keeping it accurate, accessible and enforced.
- In this policy, 'must' denotes a mandatory requirement, 'should' denotes a strong recommendation, and 'may' denotes a permitted option.
- Where this policy conflicts with applicable law, the law prevails; where it is stricter than the law, this policy prevails.
- Questions about interpretation should be directed to the Policy Owner or the Compliance team.

## 1. Eligibility

### 1.1 Overview

This section sets out how Axon manages **eligibility** within the framework of the Remote and Flexible Work Policy. It explains the objective, the expectations on individuals, and the evidence required to demonstrate compliance.

### 1.2 Requirements

1.2.1 Controls for eligibility shall be reviewed annually and updated where gaps or changes in risk are identified.
1.2.2 Line managers must review any significant change to eligibility and keep an entry in the central register of the change.
1.2.3 Concerns relating to eligibility must be raised promptly through the Speak-Up channel, the manager, or the Compliance team.
1.2.4 The relevant control owner are responsible for ensuring that eligibility is understood, applied consistently and evidenced with a documented decision memo.
1.2.5 New hires and role changers must complete training covering eligibility within thirty days of start or transfer.
1.2.6 Evidence supporting eligibility must be retained according to the Records Retention Schedule and be available for audit.
1.2.7 Line managers must test all activities related to eligibility in accordance with this policy and applicable law.
1.2.8 Any exception relating to eligibility requires a signed approval approved by the Policy Owner before the activity proceeds.
1.2.9 Where local law sets a stricter requirement for eligibility, the stricter requirement prevails in that jurisdiction.
1.2.10 Failure to follow the requirements for eligibility may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.
1.2.11 Decisions about eligibility must be proportionate, risk-based and consistent with Axon's values.
1.2.12 Metrics for eligibility will be collected quarterly and presented to the governance committee.
1.2.13 Axon expects every person involved in eligibility to act with honesty, diligence and transparency.
1.2.14 Axon will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a good-faith concern about eligibility.
1.2.15 The internal audit function shall approve eligibility monthly and report the outcome to senior management.
1.2.16 Where eligibility involves a third party, the relevant control owner must confirm that the third party meets equivalent standards.

### 1.3 Responsibilities

- **The executive leadership team** must document and report on eligibility at least once every twelve months.
- **The relevant control owner** must communicate and document eligibility monthly.
- **Line managers** must maintain and document eligibility quarterly.
- **Department heads** must communicate and report on eligibility semi-annually.
- **All employees** must validate and approve eligibility semi-annually.

### 1.4 Controls Checklist

- [ ] Confirm that eligibility is communicated and supported by a written record.
- [ ] Confirm that eligibility is documented and supported by a written record.
- [ ] Confirm that eligibility is documented and supported by an entry in the central register.
- [ ] Confirm that eligibility is documented and supported by a signed approval.
- [ ] Confirm that eligibility is documented and supported by a signed approval.
- [ ] Confirm that eligibility is tested and supported by a ticket in the case management system.
- [ ] Confirm that eligibility is approved and supported by an auditable log entry.
- [ ] Confirm that eligibility is monitored and supported by a documented decision memo.

### 1.5 Guidance and Examples

- *Example 1:* An employee facing a question about eligibility should seek Compliance advice before acting, and should keep a ticket in the case management system of the outcome.
- *Example 2:* An employee facing a question about eligibility should contact the Policy Owner before acting, and should keep a documented decision memo of the outcome.
- *Example 3:* An employee facing a question about eligibility should seek Compliance advice before acting, and should keep an auditable log entry of the outcome.
- *Example 4:* An employee facing a question about eligibility should raise a ticket before acting, and should keep a signed approval of the outcome.
- *Example 5:* An employee facing a question about eligibility should consult their manager before acting, and should keep a written record of the outcome.
