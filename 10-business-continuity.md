## Preamble

- This Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Policy establishes the standards Axon applies in the area of business continuity and disaster recovery.
- It applies to every employee, officer, director, contractor, consultant and temporary worker acting for Axon in any country.
- The Operations & Risk owns this document and is accountable for keeping it accurate, accessible and enforced.
- In this policy, 'must' denotes a mandatory requirement, 'should' denotes a strong recommendation, and 'may' denotes a permitted option.
- Where this policy conflicts with applicable law, the law prevails; where it is stricter than the law, this policy prevails.
- Questions about interpretation should be directed to the Policy Owner or the Compliance team.

## 1. Continuity Governance

### 1.1 Overview

This section sets out how Axon manages **continuity governance** within the framework of the Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Policy. It explains the objective, the expectations on individuals, and the evidence required to demonstrate compliance.

### 1.2 Requirements

1.2.1 Decisions about continuity governance must be proportionate, risk-based and consistent with Axon's values.
1.2.2 Where continuity governance involves a third party, the relevant control owner must confirm that the third party meets equivalent standards.
1.2.3 Metrics for continuity governance will be collected whenever material changes occur and presented to the governance committee.
1.2.4 The internal audit function must escalate any significant change to continuity governance and keep a documented decision memo of the change.
1.2.5 Failure to follow the requirements for continuity governance may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.
1.2.6 The relevant control owner must document all activities related to continuity governance in accordance with this policy and applicable law.
1.2.7 New hires and role changers must complete training covering continuity governance within thirty days of start or transfer.
1.2.8 Any exception relating to continuity governance requires a ticket in the case management system approved by the Policy Owner before the activity proceeds.
1.2.9 Controls for continuity governance shall be reviewed semi-annually and updated where gaps or changes in risk are identified.
1.2.10 Contractors and temporary staff are responsible for ensuring that continuity governance is understood, applied consistently and evidenced with a written record.
1.2.11 Concerns relating to continuity governance must be raised promptly through the Speak-Up channel, the manager, or the Compliance team.
1.2.12 Where local law sets a stricter requirement for continuity governance, the stricter requirement prevails in that jurisdiction.
1.2.13 Axon will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a good-faith concern about continuity governance.
1.2.14 Axon expects every person involved in continuity governance to act with honesty, diligence and transparency.
1.2.15 Evidence supporting continuity governance must be retained according to the Records Retention Schedule and be available for audit.
1.2.16 Department heads shall escalate continuity governance quarterly and report the outcome to senior management.

### 1.3 Responsibilities

- **The relevant control owner** must maintain and validate continuity governance at least once every twelve months.
- **The legal department** must maintain and document continuity governance whenever material changes occur.
- **The executive leadership team** must review and validate continuity governance whenever material changes occur.
- **Department heads** must test and test continuity governance whenever material changes occur.
- **The policy owner** must escalate and approve continuity governance monthly.

### 1.4 Controls Checklist

- [ ] Confirm that continuity governance is communicated and supported by a signed approval.
- [ ] Confirm that continuity governance is documented and supported by a ticket in the case management system.
- [ ] Confirm that continuity governance is communicated and supported by a ticket in the case management system.
- [ ] Confirm that continuity governance is reviewed and supported by a written record.
- [ ] Confirm that continuity governance is documented and supported by an auditable log entry.
- [ ] Confirm that continuity governance is communicated and supported by an auditable log entry.
- [ ] Confirm that continuity governance is reviewed and supported by a signed approval.
- [ ] Confirm that continuity governance is reviewed and supported by a written record.

### 1.5 Guidance and Examples

- *Example 1:* An employee facing a question about continuity governance should contact the Policy Owner before acting, and should keep an entry in the central register of the outcome.
