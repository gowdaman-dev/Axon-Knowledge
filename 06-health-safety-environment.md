## Preamble

- This Health, Safety and Environment Policy establishes the standards Axon applies in the area of health, safety and environment.
- It applies to every employee, officer, director, contractor, consultant and temporary worker acting for Axon in any country.
- The Facilities & EHS owns this document and is accountable for keeping it accurate, accessible and enforced.
- In this policy, 'must' denotes a mandatory requirement, 'should' denotes a strong recommendation, and 'may' denotes a permitted option.
- Where this policy conflicts with applicable law, the law prevails; where it is stricter than the law, this policy prevails.
- Questions about interpretation should be directed to the Policy Owner or the Compliance team.

## 1. Safety Leadership

### 1.1 Overview

This section sets out how Axon manages **safety leadership** within the framework of the Health, Safety and Environment Policy. It explains the objective, the expectations on individuals, and the evidence required to demonstrate compliance.

### 1.2 Requirements

1.2.1 Failure to follow the requirements for safety leadership may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.
1.2.2 Decisions about safety leadership must be proportionate, risk-based and consistent with Axon's values.
1.2.3 Axon will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a good-faith concern about safety leadership.
1.2.4 Where safety leadership involves a third party, department heads must confirm that the third party meets equivalent standards.
1.2.5 Axon expects every person involved in safety leadership to act with honesty, diligence and transparency.
1.2.6 The policy owner must document any significant change to safety leadership and keep a written record of the change.
1.2.7 Evidence supporting safety leadership must be retained according to the Records Retention Schedule and be available for audit.
1.2.8 All employees shall test safety leadership semi-annually and report the outcome to senior management.
1.2.9 New hires and role changers must complete training covering safety leadership within thirty days of start or transfer.
1.2.10 Where local law sets a stricter requirement for safety leadership, the stricter requirement prevails in that jurisdiction.
1.2.11 Metrics for safety leadership will be collected monthly and presented to the governance committee.
1.2.12 Any exception relating to safety leadership requires an auditable log entry approved by the Policy Owner before the activity proceeds.
1.2.13 Line managers are responsible for ensuring that safety leadership is understood, applied consistently and evidenced with a documented decision memo.
1.2.14 Contractors and temporary staff must communicate all activities related to safety leadership in accordance with this policy and applicable law.
1.2.15 Concerns relating to safety leadership must be raised promptly through the Speak-Up channel, the manager, or the Compliance team.
1.2.16 Controls for safety leadership shall be reviewed semi-annually and updated where gaps or changes in risk are identified.

### 1.3 Responsibilities

- **The internal audit function** must communicate and validate safety leadership at least once every twelve months.
- **Line managers** must escalate and document safety leadership monthly.
- **The policy owner** must escalate and report on safety leadership whenever material changes occur.
- **The compliance team** must review and validate safety leadership at least once every twelve months.
- **The legal department** must approve and approve safety leadership semi-annually.

### 1.4 Controls Checklist

- [ ] Confirm that safety leadership is escalated where needed and supported by an entry in the central register.
- [ ] Confirm that safety leadership is tested and supported by an auditable log entry.
- [ ] Confirm that safety leadership is monitored and supported by an auditable log entry.
- [ ] Confirm that safety leadership is documented and supported by a written record.
- [ ] Confirm that safety leadership is evidenced and supported by an entry in the central register.
- [ ] Confirm that safety leadership is communicated and supported by an entry in the central register.
- [ ] Confirm that safety leadership is documented and supported by an entry in the central register.
- [ ] Confirm that safety leadership is approved and supported by a documented decision memo.

### 1.5 Guidance and Examples

- *Example 1:* An employee facing a question about safety leadership should check the intranet FAQ before acting, and should keep a ticket in the case management system of the outcome.
- *Example 2:* An employee facing a question about safety leadership should raise a ticket before acting, and should keep an entry in the central register of the outcome.
- *Example 3:* An employee facing a question about safety leadership should read this section again before acting, and should keep a signed approval of the outcome.
- *Example 4:* An employee facing a question about safety leadership should consult their manager before acting, and should keep a signed approval of the outcome.
- *Example 5:* An employee facing a question about safety leadership should contact the Policy Owner before acting, and should keep a written record of the outcome.
