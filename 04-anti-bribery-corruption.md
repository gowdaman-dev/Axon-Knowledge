## Preamble

- This Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption Policy establishes the standards Axon applies in the area of anti-bribery and anti-corruption.
- It applies to every employee, officer, director, contractor, consultant and temporary worker acting for Axon in any country.
- The Legal & Compliance owns this document and is accountable for keeping it accurate, accessible and enforced.
- In this policy, 'must' denotes a mandatory requirement, 'should' denotes a strong recommendation, and 'may' denotes a permitted option.
- Where this policy conflicts with applicable law, the law prevails; where it is stricter than the law, this policy prevails.
- Questions about interpretation should be directed to the Policy Owner or the Compliance team.

## 1. Zero Tolerance Statement

### 1.1 Overview

This section sets out how Axon manages **zero tolerance statement** within the framework of the Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption Policy. It explains the objective, the expectations on individuals, and the evidence required to demonstrate compliance.

### 1.2 Requirements

1.2.1 Where zero tolerance statement involves a third party, the relevant control owner must confirm that the third party meets equivalent standards.
1.2.2 Controls for zero tolerance statement shall be reviewed quarterly and updated where gaps or changes in risk are identified.
1.2.3 Where local law sets a stricter requirement for zero tolerance statement, the stricter requirement prevails in that jurisdiction.
1.2.4 Any exception relating to zero tolerance statement requires a written record approved by the Policy Owner before the activity proceeds.
1.2.5 The relevant control owner shall approve zero tolerance statement whenever material changes occur and report the outcome to senior management.
1.2.6 Failure to follow the requirements for zero tolerance statement may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.
1.2.7 Evidence supporting zero tolerance statement must be retained according to the Records Retention Schedule and be available for audit.
1.2.8 Department heads must escalate all activities related to zero tolerance statement in accordance with this policy and applicable law.
1.2.9 Axon expects every person involved in zero tolerance statement to act with honesty, diligence and transparency.
1.2.10 Concerns relating to zero tolerance statement must be raised promptly through the Speak-Up channel, the manager, or the Compliance team.
1.2.11 New hires and role changers must complete training covering zero tolerance statement within thirty days of start or transfer.
1.2.12 Axon will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a good-faith concern about zero tolerance statement.
1.2.13 Contractors and temporary staff are responsible for ensuring that zero tolerance statement is understood, applied consistently and evidenced with a signed approval.
1.2.14 The executive leadership team must review any significant change to zero tolerance statement and keep a written record of the change.
1.2.15 Decisions about zero tolerance statement must be proportionate, risk-based and consistent with Axon's values.
1.2.16 Metrics for zero tolerance statement will be collected monthly and presented to the governance committee.

### 1.3 Responsibilities

- **The internal audit function** must monitor and monitor zero tolerance statement monthly.
- **The legal department** must approve and report on zero tolerance statement whenever material changes occur.
- **All employees** must escalate and maintain zero tolerance statement quarterly.
- **Contractors and temporary staff** must report on and maintain zero tolerance statement at least once every twelve months.
- **The executive leadership team** must review and maintain zero tolerance statement monthly.

### 1.4 Controls Checklist

- [ ] Confirm that zero tolerance statement is tested and supported by an auditable log entry.
- [ ] Confirm that zero tolerance statement is evidenced and supported by a ticket in the case management system.
- [ ] Confirm that zero tolerance statement is tested and supported by an entry in the central register.
- [ ] Confirm that zero tolerance statement is monitored and supported by a ticket in the case management system.
- [ ] Confirm that zero tolerance statement is reviewed and supported by an auditable log entry.
- [ ] Confirm that zero tolerance statement is communicated and supported by an entry in the central register.
- [ ] Confirm that zero tolerance statement is monitored and supported by an auditable log entry.
